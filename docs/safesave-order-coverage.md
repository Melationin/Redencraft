## SafeSave contracts that are not vanilla behavior

- `ServerContracts.java:79–104` starts with live stone ticks at `(2,87,2)` and `(3,87,2)`, adds a saved duplicate of the first with trigger `now+4`, priority -1, suborder 100 plus two invalid IDs, and restores at a rebased time. The assertion expects two ticks, saved duplicate at `now+104`, suborder 100 and priority -1, and two dropped IDs. `ScheduledTickManager.java:88–132` explains replacement followed by rescheduling live ticks: the saved duplicate wins. This is SafeSave recovery and conflict policy. Vanilla `SavedTick.java:27–48` stores a **relative delay**, priority and position/type, and reconstructs suborder at load, so do not require SafeSave's original global suborder from ordinary Anvil NBT.
- `ServerContracts.java:105–115` adds saved note events `(2,87,2,0,1,100)`, `(3,87,2,0,2,101)`, and unknown `(4,87,2,0,3,102)`, expecting two events, the original duplicate's live order, and sequence at least 102. The merge/unknown-ID behavior is SafeSave; ordinary runtime event equality and insertion order can be tested separately.
- `ServerRegression.java:449–465` verifies rebasing `(120,100,1000)→1020`, `(80,100,1000)→980`, monotonic restored sequence, SafeSave chunk NBT round trip, and missing-data fallback. These tests concern mod metadata, not vanilla tick execution. `ServerRegression.java:106–150,161–180` adds frozen startup, restored chunk readiness, player gating, tickets, and reactivation: all are recovery orchestration, not C++ world-loading requirements.
- The trace collects entity, block, fluid, event, hopper, and piston callbacks; per-dimension groups are sorted but order **within** each dimension is retained (`ServerRegression.java:432–440`). Entity and fluid simulation, hopper inventory, saving and recovery, tickets, and multiple dimensions are outside this project’s current scope. They are **not tested**, not failed C++ comparisons. Unsupported block-state semantics should likewise be reported rather than interpreted as parity.

## Evidence status

This is a source mapping and comparison plan, not a parity result. Existing C++ tests are regression assertions against the current implementation; no SafeSave2 run or clean Minecraft 26.1 differential was performed in this review. A valid differential needs equivalent initial world state, loaded chunks, game time, explicit tick/event insertion order and trace points on an unmodified 26.1 server; separately label any SafeSave-modified trace and do not compare restart-only metadata to vanilla.
